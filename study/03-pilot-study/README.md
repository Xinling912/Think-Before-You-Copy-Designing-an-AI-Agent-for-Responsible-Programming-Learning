# 03 Pilot Study

The pilot study was a small descriptive evaluation with six undergraduate
participants. It examined perceived support for guided thinking, reliability
judgment, programming understanding, interaction clarity, and future use.

## Public materials

- [`study-design/`](study-design/) — pilot plan and evaluation scope;
- [`original-questionnaire/`](original-questionnaire/) — bilingual questionnaire
  and static interface snapshot;
- [`analysis-script/`](analysis-script/) — analysis workflow and reporting rules;
- [`analysis-result/`](analysis-result/) — aggregate rating summaries, figures,
  and theme-level qualitative results.

The participant-level six-person export and the OSF ZIP are not mirrored to
public GitHub. With such a small sample, pseudonyms alone do not eliminate the
risk of contextual re-identification. Results are preliminary and descriptive;
they are not evidence of causal learning gains.
